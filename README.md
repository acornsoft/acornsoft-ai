# Luna Foundry Multiagent

Every meaningful piece of work is a **climb**. There is a summit worth standing on—a working product, a client delivery, a decision you can defend—and a trail between here and there. Fog rolls in. Side trails look easier. Gear multiplies. Without a shared story of the route, people get tired, tools disagree, and “almost there” never becomes the summit.

That is the **Climb Notes** way of seeing the world: **mountain first, kit second**. Name the peak. Walk Base Camp → route → checkpoints → summit push. Anyone should be able to follow the story of the climb without a dump of tools on page one. **Luna Foundry Multiagent** is how that story becomes how you actually work with AI—day after day, on whatever ridge you already stand on.

On the mountain you are not alone. **Luna** is your **Sherpa**. She does not carry you or replace your judgment. She brings **guidelines, direction, tools, waypoints, and planning** so the rope team stays coherent. Along the route she introduces a simple, memorable rhythm—**MacroFlow**—that maps to how real climbs feel:

- **Base Camp** — Are we fit to leave? Gear, weather, rules of the mountain, who we are on this trip.  
- **Route** — One summit objective, clear success marks, what we are *not* climbing today.  
- **Waypoint** — Check the map. Stop, look around, recover if needed, then decide: hold or go.  
- **Summit** — The rope team moves: build, prove, bring evidence home—not just a story of the attempt.  

What Multiagent **gives you** is that climb made real: one pack of companions and practices, the same route language wherever you think, and a proper camp for your hands (shell and proof next to the work). What it **spares you** is thrash—half-packed bags, three dialects for three tools, and summits claimed without a photo of the cairn. You choose the mountain; Luna keeps the path human and the arrival undeniable.
