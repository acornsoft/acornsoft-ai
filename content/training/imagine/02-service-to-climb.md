# Imagine · 02 — Service to climb

**Tool:** Grok Imagine  
**Time:** ~10 minutes  
**Done when:** One graphic shows how a service leaves a Climb Note.

## Goal
Connect “what we sell” to “what we write.”

## Do this
1. Pick one service (e.g. Product Build).  
2. Imagine a two-panel image: left = service action, right = Climb Note page (P/M/S/L).  
3. Title: “Every climb leaves a trail.”  
4. Use on `/service` long-form or X as a still with a link to the site.

## Done when
- The link between service work and Climb Notes is obvious.  
- You can reuse the frame for other services.
