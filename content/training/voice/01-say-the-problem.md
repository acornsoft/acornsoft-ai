# Voice · 01 — Name Base Camp

**Tool:** Grok Voice (Luna)  
**Time:** ~5 minutes  
**Done when:** You can say whether you are fit to leave in one breath.

## Goal
Stop starting with tools. Start with who you are on this trip.

## Do this
1. Open Voice. Ask Luna: “Help me name Base Camp — not the tools.”  
2. Answer in plain words. Avoid product names first.  
3. Repeat until Luna can restate who you are, the gear, and whether you are fit to leave.  
4. Write that sentence as **Base Camp** on a draft Climb Note.

## Done when
- The sentence names a person and whether the climb can start.  
- You did not lead with a model or app list.
