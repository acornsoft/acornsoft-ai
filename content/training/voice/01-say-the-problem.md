# Voice · 01 — Say the problem

**Tool:** Grok Voice (Luna)  
**Time:** ~5 minutes  
**Done when:** You can state a clearly defined problem in one breath.

## Goal
Stop starting with tools. Start with what’s stuck.

## Do this
1. Open Voice. Ask Luna: “Help me name what’s stuck — not the tools.”  
2. Answer in plain words. Avoid product names first.  
3. Repeat until Luna can restate your problem in one short sentence.  
4. Write that sentence as **Problem** on a draft Climb Note.

## Done when
- The sentence names a person and a stuck outcome.  
- You did not lead with a model or app list.
