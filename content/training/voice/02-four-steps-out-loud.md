# Voice · 02 — Four steps out loud

**Tool:** Grok Voice (Luna)  
**Time:** ~10 minutes  
**Done when:** You can say Problem, Measure, Slice, Lesson without notes.

## Goal
Make the climb muscle memory.

## Do this
1. Pick Climb Note 000 (or any public note).  
2. Ask Luna to quiz you on the four steps in order.  
3. Answer out loud. Fix weak steps.  
4. Apply the same four steps to **your** problem from module 01.

## Done when
- You can list the four steps and one plain phrase for each.  
- You have a spoken draft of Measure and Slice for your climb.
