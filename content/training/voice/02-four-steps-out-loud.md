# Voice · 02 — Four beats out loud

**Tool:** Grok Voice (Luna)  
**Time:** ~10 minutes  
**Done when:** You can say Base Camp, Route, Waypoint, Summit without notes.

## Goal
Make the climb muscle memory.

## Do this
1. Pick Climb Note 000 (or any public note).  
2. Ask Luna to quiz you on the four beats in order.  
3. Answer out loud. Fix weak beats.  
4. Apply the same four beats to **your** Base Camp from module 01.

## Done when
- You can list the four beats and one plain phrase for each.  
- You have a spoken draft of Route and Waypoint for your climb.
