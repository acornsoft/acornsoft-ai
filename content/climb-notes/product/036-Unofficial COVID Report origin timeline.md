## Base Camp
Public COVID claims and records lived in a pile of tabs. You could not walk from origin to now in one place. You could not put a claim next to the record with sources.

## Route
Someone opens one site. They jump year by year from 2019. They can check a claim against the record with sources — without a research desk.

## Waypoint
Grok Build ships the timeline from this Climb Note. Grok Bot hunts public sources. Imagine shows the path before we build. Grok Voice can walk a visitor through a year.

Result: [https://unofficial-covid-report.acornsoft.ai/](https://unofficial-covid-report.acornsoft.ai/)

## Summit
