## Base Camp

You keep a private list of “someday.” Learn the language. Fix the money loop. Call the friend. Start the small business. Write the story your kids will actually hear. That is who you are on this trip: a person with hope and no trail. You are not fit to leave until one someday can be said out loud.

## Route

One summit: one real someday item moves into this week with a finished first step. Success: this existed only as a wish on Monday; by Sunday it has a first footprint. Not today: a reinvention of your life, or a thirty-day overhaul.

## Waypoint

Check the map. The list is still a folder of good intentions. Recover if the week stole the hour. Hold or go: go — name one someday out loud.

## Summit

Write it as a Climb Note. Share it with one person if you want, or keep it private. Proof: a trail you can find again. Someday becomes real when it has a map — not a story of the attempt.
