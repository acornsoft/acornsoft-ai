## Base Camp

Payday feels fine. Two weeks later the account is thin and you cannot quite say where it went. Shame keeps you from looking closely. That is who you are on this trip: a person with a quiet leak. You are not fit to leave until you can look without turning away.

## Route

One summit: for the next full pay cycle you can point to where every major outflow went. Success: one automatic or scheduled move puts money on your side before the leak starts. Not today: a full budget hobby, or a new finance app.

## Waypoint

Check the map. The same pattern is about to repeat. Recover: drop the shame long enough to look. Hold or go: go — one Climb Note, one pay cycle.

## Summit

Set three buckets: must-pay, life, future-you. Make one transfer or rule on payday before anything else. Proof: you can point to the trail, and name what surprised you when you finally looked. Money with no map will always look like it disappeared.
