## Problem

Payday feels fine. Two weeks later the account is thin and you cannot quite say where it went. Not a crisis every month — a quiet leak. Shame keeps you from looking closely, so the same pattern repeats.

## Measure

For the next full pay cycle you can point to where every major outflow went — and you have one automatic or scheduled move that puts money on your side before the leak starts.

## Slice

One Climb Note, one pay cycle. Problem = the feeling of money vanishing without a story. Measure = a simple trail (three buckets is enough: must-pay, life, future-you). Slice = one transfer or rule on payday before anything else. Lesson = what surprised you when you finally looked.

## Lesson

Money that has no map will always look like it disappeared. A Climb Note is not a spreadsheet hobby — it is a trail so next month is not a mystery again.
