## Base Camp
Leaving the enterprise alone is lonely. Building a studio that still burns people out is the same mountain with a new logo. Thriving means pace, ownership, and climbs people are proud to sign.

## Route
Teammates can name their climbs, their rest rules, and how Climb Notes capture credit — without a corporate performance theater.

## Waypoint
Define roles as climbs, not titles. Every engagement leaves a Climb Note. Protect deep-work blocks. Hire only people who want the mountaineering metaphor, not just the exit story.

## Summit
A top-notch AI-first studio is measured by whether people thrive on the path — not by how much corporate residue they still carry.
