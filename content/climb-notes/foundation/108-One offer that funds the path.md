## Base Camp
Ten half-offers feel busy and fund nothing. A new studio dies from optionality: everything is possible, nothing is sold clearly, cash arrives late or never.

## Route
One named offer with a price path, a first-delivery Climb Note template, and a yes/no fit rule you can say in thirty seconds.

## Waypoint
Ship the offer page and the first delivery Climb Note template. Take one real conversation all the way to a clear yes or no.

## Summit
Funding a studio is a climb with one pitch at a time. Clarity is the rope; variety without a flagship is fog.
