## Feedback (in-product)

Stay in the climb. Ask **Luna** (or your host partner) to capture a **skill or feature wish** as a short brief — OS, what you ran (Setup, Grok Build, Copilot, shell), and what failed or should improve. **No secrets** in the brief.

You can also send a Climb Note: **https://www.acornsoft.ai/start**

Keep that brief in your workspace (Climb Notes, `docs/Analysis/outputs/`, or chat). Maintainers collect wishes from those packs; there is no public issue tracker required for normal use.
