Stay in the climb. **[Send a Climb Note](https://www.acornsoft.ai/start)** or ask **Luna** to capture a **skill or feature wish** — OS, what you ran (Setup, Grok Build, Copilot, shell), and what failed or should improve. **No secrets** in the brief.
